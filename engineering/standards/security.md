# Security, privacy, and supply chain

Required considerations:

- explicit authorized scope and trust-boundary mapping
- risk-calibrated quick, standard, or deep assessment from engineering/security/assessment-contract.md
- static, structural, secret, dependency/misconfiguration, and authorized local dynamic checks
- candidate -> validated/rejected/blocked finding lifecycle with stable root-cause deduplication
- severity grounded in demonstrated confidentiality, integrity, availability, or business impact
- identity, access, secret management, privacy, retention, auditability, compliance, provenance, SBOM, and secure defaults
- non-secret reproducible evidence, minimum remediation, remaining risk, and independent ENG-15 verification

Applicable work must record commands, environment, revision, evidence, limitations, and remaining risk.
